# Sea Captain

## Investment

- **Minimal:** 16★
- **Optimal:** awk2
- **High-End:** awk3

## Usage

- **PvE:** No %dmg or true dmg unless has the 2nd core => bad in PVE
- **PvP:** ok
- **Bossing:** Not recommended

## Build

- **Pets:** Mystic Witch
- **Runes:**

  | Type     | Important                                                                                                              |
  | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Attack   | Attack, Armor Penetration, Physical Damage Increase, Damage Increase, Knockback, Lifesteal                             |
  | Effect   | Knockback Resistance, Movement Speed, ...                                                                              |
  | Energy   | Energy Regen when attacked, ATK Energy Regen, ATK Energy Reduction, Energy Increase, Energy Regen, Energy Surplus, ... |
  | Survival | Damage Reduction, Ranged Damage Reduction, ...                                                                         |

- **Core:** all are usable, prio: 1st, 4th, 3rd, 2nd
- **Relic:** Shield/Boost
